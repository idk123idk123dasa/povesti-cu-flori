  • 2 letters per months for 12 months
  • 1 postcard is included in every other letter

Our Debut collection takes place during WWII and is centered on one of the most significant days in history: June 6, 1944 . . . D-Day.

Audrey Rose Drollinger meets Corporal Charlie Henderson Burke at a Fourth of July Army Ranger dance in Tullahoma Tennessee. The two soon learn they have a great deal in common: a love for their country, loyalty to the army, and, of course, an immediate attraction to one another.

As their relationship develops through their correspondence, the couple moves closer and closer to one of the most important days of WWII. What neither foresees, however, is the significant role each will play in the day’s events . . . and whether they’ll both make it out alive.

Subscriber will be charged $12 each month, for 12 months. Cancel your subscription at any time.
