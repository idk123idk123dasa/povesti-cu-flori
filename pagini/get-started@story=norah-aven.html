Întrebări frecvente

Ce sunt Scrisori Cu Povesti? ▾
Scrisori Cu Povesti este o experiență de poveste de 12 luni, livrată prin 24 de scrisori frumos ilustrate trimise direct la ușa ta - două pe lună pentru un an întreg. Fiecare poveste este plasată într-o eră istorică diferită, plină de dragoste, aventură și intrigi.
How do I give this as a gift? ▾
Simply enter your recipient's name and shipping address at checkout — the letters will be mailed directly to them. You'll also receive a free printable gift card you can download and print to present in person, or email digitally to let them know their story is on the way.
What will I receive? ▾
Each envelope contains a handcrafted story letter plus extras like postcards, telegrams, newspaper clippings, maps, and more — all designed to make the story feel real and immersive. You'll receive 24 letters over 12 months.
Do you ship internationally? ▾
Yes! However, international orders are sent as a complete set of letters delivered all at once, rather than mailed two per month, due to international shipping costs and constraints.
When will my letters arrive? ▾
Your first letter ships via USPS Ground Advantage and typically arrives within 2–5 business days. Regular mailings typically arrive within 2 weeks of each mailing date. View our mailing schedule →

Întrebări frecvente

What's the difference between the plans? ▾
The Poveste Preplătită + Cutie includes all 24 letters plus a matching keepsake tin — your first letter arrives inside the tin, making it a beautiful gift presentation. Poveste Preplătită is the same 24 letters without the tin, paid upfront at a savings. Monthly is the same experience paid month by month — cancel anytime.
Is this a recurring charge? ▾
The Poveste Preplătită + Cutie and Poveste Preplătită are one-time payments — you pay once and receive all 24 letters over the year with no further charges. Monthly is a recurring monthly charge for 12 months, and you can cancel anytime.
When will the first letter arrive? ▾
You choose your ship date at checkout. Once shipped, your first letter typically arrives within 2–5 business days via USPS Ground Advantage.
Can I cancel anytime? ▾
Monthly subscribers can cancel anytime. If you choose Poveste Preplătită or Poveste Preplătită + Cutie, you're covered by our 30-day money-back guarantee — if you're not happy after the first two letters, we'll refund you in full, no questions asked.
What if they don't like it? ▾
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If your recipient isn't happy after the first two letters, just let us know and we'll issue a full refund — no questions asked.
Can I change the shipping address after ordering? ▾
Yes — contact us at suport@scrisoricupovesti.ro and we'll update it before the next mailing.
